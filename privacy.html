Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026. Applies to Nestra 1.3.0 and later.
The short version.

Nestra works on your PC. We never receive your clipboard, your files, your captures or your recordings.

No accountsNo telemetryNo analyticsNo ads

Nestra is a shelf, clipboard history and screen capture tool for Windows. This policy explains what Nestra touches on your PC, where it keeps it, and how you control it.

1. What Nestra accesses and why

Clipboard history

  • Clipboard history is stored only on this device, in Nestra's app data folder. It is never synced.
  • You control it in Settings › Clipboard.
    • Pause capture stops recording new clips.
    • Excluded apps never records copies made in the apps you choose.
    • History length keeps the last 50, 100 or 500 clips. Older clips are deleted. Pinned clips are kept until you unpin or remove them.
    • You can remove any clip, and turn off Show Clipboard in the shelf.
  • Copies that a password manager marks as private are always skipped. Nestra respects the standard Windows signals for this.
  • Text that looks like a password or API key is shown masked on screen until you hover it.

Screen, microphone and OCR

  • Nestra uses the screen only after you start a capture, and the microphone only while the record bar is open with the mic on. It does not watch your screen or listen in the background.
  • Text recognition uses Windows OCR, which runs on your PC.
  • Voice cleanup for recordings runs on your PC.

What Nestra does not do

  • It does not record keystrokes. While you draw on a recording, it watches for Delete, Backspace and V so you can edit the drawing. Nothing is stored.
  • It does not collect usage data, crash reports, device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • It does not sell, share or send your information to us or anyone else.

2. Network use

Nestra has no server and does not phone home. It makes one kind of network request, and only when you act.

  • Web images. When you drag or paste a web image or an image link onto the shelf, Nestra downloads the original image from the website that hosts it, so you keep a sharp copy. The request goes only to that website, which sees your IP address as it would for any visit. It names Nestra as the app and the website itself as the page you came from.

Email us in Settings opens your own email app. Nothing else in Nestra uses the internet.

3. Diagnostics

Nestra keeps small local log files in its app data folder to help fix problems. They never leave your PC on their own.

Settings › About Nestra › Export diagnostics saves one diagnostics file to your Desktop and copies it, so you can paste it into a support email. It contains your Nestra version, Windows version, screen setup and recent technical log lines. It does not contain clipboard contents, file or folder names, or your Windows user name. Microphone names keep only their hardware words, such as “Headset AirPods”. It leaves your device only if you choose to send it to us.

4. Storage and security

All data stays on your PC under your Windows user account, protected by Windows' own account and file permissions. Nestra does not upload, back up or sync any of it.

5. Deleting your data

  • A single clip or item. Remove it in Nestra.
  • Old clips. Lower the History length.
  • Everything. Uninstall Nestra. The Microsoft Store version removes all of its app data, including clipboard history and shelf items. The direct-download version asks whether to remove them too.
  • Copies made for dragging into web apps are temporary. Nestra removes them each time it quits or starts.
  • Screenshots and recordings are saved as normal files in your Pictures and Videos folders. They stay after uninstall so you don't lose your work. Delete them there like any other file.

6. Children

Nestra is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

7. Changes

If a new version of Nestra changes what it accesses or how, we will update this policy and the date at the top before that version is released.
