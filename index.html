Questions? Answers.

How much does Nestra cost?

$39.99, once. No subscription. Updates come free through the Microsoft Store.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Every feature is free for 15 days. Buy it from the Store whenever you're ready to keep it.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Install it and it's ready.

Where is my data kept?

On your PC only. Nestra never sends your clipboard, files, captures or recordings anywhere. Read the privacy policy.

Which Windows do I need?

Windows 10 version 2004 or later, or Windows 11. It looks its best on Windows 11.

How do I get help?

Email support@thenestra.app. You can attach a diagnostics file from Settings › About Nestra, and it never includes your clipboard or file names.
